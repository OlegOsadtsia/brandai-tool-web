Pro Tips for Picking the Right Domain

Keep it Short

Aim for 6-12 characters. Short names are easier to remember and type, reducing the risk of typos for your customers.

Stick to .com

While there are many extensions, .com remains the gold standard for trust and global recognition in business.

Avoid Hyphens

Numbers and hyphens often confuse people when they hear your brand name. Stay purely alphabetic for better recall.
